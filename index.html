Every brief is an opportunity to grow. I want to make the most of it, so I lead with ambition rather than pressure.

I show people what the work can be and give them the confidence to find a way to make it. My proudest moment is when someone on the team takes a leap. And I’ll do my best to help them land.

Taught Me
It’s Worth Asking
Why Are They Watching

I grew up in Budapest, studied in Glasgow, and built a career in London. Sociology and psychology gave me a way of seeing. Filmmaking gave me a way of expressing it.

Freelancing taught me that craft alone isn’t enough. Working with entrepreneurs showed me what resilience and intentionality look like. Seeing the winners on stage at Cannes set the bar of where I need to be.

The Lions Creative MBA put a framework behind it. Now I lead a team where that thinking sits at the centre of our creative decisions.
